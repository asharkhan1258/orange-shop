Navel & Valencia Oranges

Two orange types, one focused product story.

Our Oranges

Fresh, bright and easy to understand.

This page keeps the product range intentionally simple. Navels are known for convenient eating and easy peeling, while Valencias are appreciated for their high juice content and balanced citrus flavor.

Orange Only Quality Focused

Navel Oranges

Navels are a popular choice for fresh eating thanks to their sweet flavor, easy-peel skin and generally seedless character. Their convenient segments make them well suited for lunchboxes, snacking and fresh-cut presentations.

Key Attributes
  • Sweet, approachable flavor
  • Easy to peel and segment
  • Excellent for fresh eating

Valencia Oranges

Valencias are especially valued for juicing because of their abundant juice and refreshing citrus profile. They can also be enjoyed fresh and work well in recipes, drinks and foodservice applications.

Key Attributes
  • Excellent juice yield
  • Refreshing citrus taste
  • Versatile for foodservice
Handling & Enjoyment

From snack to fresh-squeezed juice.

For fresh eating, store oranges in a cool, dry place for short-term use or refrigerate for longer freshness. Wash before cutting. For juice, chill the fruit first for a refreshing result and serve promptly after squeezing.
