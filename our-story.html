Our Story

A focused history for Gencon Consulting Inc., centered entirely on oranges.

A Pioneering Spirit

Built around focus, consistency and relationships.

Gencon Consulting Inc. was formed on 01/02/1982. From the beginning, the company story presented here is intentionally focused on one category: oranges. The goal is to serve customers with clear product knowledge, dependable quality expectations and a practical field-to-market mindset.

Rather than trying to be everything to everyone, the brand position is simple: understand oranges well, communicate honestly and support partners with useful information from season planning through merchandising.
